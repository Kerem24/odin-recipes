Pernil Pork Food Recipe!

Description: Pernil is a classic Puerto Rican slow-roasted pork shoulder, deeply marinated overnight in garlic, oregano, citrus juice, and warm spices. It is roasted low and slow until the meat is fall-apart tender and juicy, crowned with a crackling, golden-crisp layer of skin known as cuerito.

Ingredients

  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh oregano
  • 1 tablespoon ground cumin
  • 2 teaspoons ground ancho chile pepper
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 2 teaspoons ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil, or as needed
  • 1 (3 pound) boneless pork loin roast
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Blend the garlic, onion, oregano, cumin, chile pepper, salt, pepper, vinegar, and olive oil into a paste.
  2. Coat the pork loin completely with the paste mixture.
  3. Place the coated pork into the slow cooker.
  4. Cover and cook on Low for 6 to 8 hours until fork-tender.
  5. Shred or chunk the pork, then serve garnished with lime wedges.
